*Q1. Three Greatest Benefits of AI for Humanity*

To be honest, when I first heard about AI I thought it is only for robots. But after Lesson 1 I understood its real use.

First benefit I see is *Healthcare*. In our area, there is no big hospital nearby. If AI can help doctors to find disease early by checking reports, many lives can be saved. It is very important for poor people.

Second is *Education and Farming*. Both are connected to us. A farmer can take a photo of his crop and AI can tell if there is any infection. Same for students like me at Nalanda IT Zone, we can get a personal teacher who can explain any topic in our own language, even at night.

Third is *Solving big problems*. AI can do calculations that humans cannot. It can help to make better weather prediction, manage traffic, and find new ways to save electricity. This will help the whole country.

*Q2. Three Biggest Threats of AI*

But there are also many risks.

First, *Job Loss*. This is what I worry about most. If AI does all the work of typing, calling, driving, then what will normal people do? It can increase unemployment.

Second, *Fake Information*. This is already happening. With AI anyone can make a fake video or fake news. People will not know what is true and what is fake. This can be very dangerous for our society.

Third, *No Human Control*. If we give too much power to AI, like control of weapons or bank systems, and if something goes wrong, we can't stop it. Humans must always have the final decision.

*Q3. My Personal Verdict - Net Positive or Negative?*

In my opinion, AI will be a *Net Positive* in the end.

I think AI itself is not good or bad. It depends on how we use it. Like fire and mobile phones, they also have good and bad sides, but we learned to use them.

If we students learn AI with honesty and ethics, we can make it useful for our own villages and communities. So I am hopeful, but we have to be careful
